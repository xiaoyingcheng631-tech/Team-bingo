✏️ Edit Keywords

Edits the 9 keywords of the current round only (). Applying will reset this round's board for a fresh game. Other rounds are not affected.

📋 Edit Questions

Edits the questions of the current round only (). Saving will not change any claimed squares, scores or the selected team. Other rounds are not affected.

⚙️ Team Settings

Team names and colors are shared by all Bingo pages. Squares already claimed will update automatically.

🔄 Reset Game?

Reset This Round clears the current round's () squares and scores and returns to Question 1 — other rounds keep their progress.
Reset ALL Rounds clears every Bingo page at once.
Keywords, questions, titles, team names and colors are always kept.

🗂 Bingo Pages

Each Bingo page is a complete, independent game round with its own keywords, questions, board and scores. Teams and colors are shared. Rename a round by editing its title; reorder with ⬆⬇; delete with 🗑 (click twice to confirm).
✎ opens the full page editor (title · keywords · questions). ▶ switches the live game to that round.

✎ Edit Bingo Page

Prepare this round before class: give it a title, its nine keywords and its questions. Saving only affects this page. If the keywords change, this page's board is reset; every other page keeps its data.
